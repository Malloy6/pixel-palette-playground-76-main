2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website.

Cookies help websites:

  • Remember user preferences
  • Keep users signed in
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Analyze website performance
  • Enhance security
  • Personalize user experience

Cookies do not normally contain information that personally identifies an individual, although information linked to cookies may be associated with your account information.
