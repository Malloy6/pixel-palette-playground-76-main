1. ABOUT ELIMUSPHERE

Elimusphere is a digital education platform that enables students to:

  • Book and attend live classes.
  • Access online learning materials and educational resources.
  • Complete and submit assignments.
  • Track learning progress.
  • Interact with approved teachers and tutors.
  • Participate in educational activities offered through the platform.

Elimusphere acts as a technology platform connecting learners with educational opportunities and approved educators.

2. ELIGIBILITY

2.1 Student Accounts

To use the platform, students must:

  • Provide accurate and complete registration information.
  • Maintain the security of their account credentials.
  • Use the platform only for lawful educational purposes.

2.2 Minors

Students under the age of 18 must have the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian where required by law.

Parents and guardians are responsible for monitoring a minor's use of the platform and ensuring compliance with this Agreement.

3. ACCOUNT RESPONSIBILITIES

Students agree to:

  • Keep login credentials confidential.
  • Notify Elimusphere immediately of unauthorized account access.
  • Maintain accurate personal information.
  • Use respectful and appropriate communication at all times.

Students are fully responsible for activities conducted through their accounts.

4. BOOKINGS AND ENROLLMENT

4.1 Class Booking

Students may book classes through the platform subject to teacher availability.

A booking is considered confirmed once:

  • Payment has been successfully processed, where applicable; and
  • A booking confirmation is issued through the platform.

4.2 Attendance

Students are responsible for:

  • Logging into scheduled classes on time.
  • Ensuring access to a stable internet connection.
  • Having the necessary devices and learning materials.

Repeated absence may affect learning progress and completion records.

5. FEES AND PAYMENTS

Students agree to pay all applicable fees for:

  • Live classes;
  • Learning programs;
  • Educational resources;
  • Premium platform services; and
  • Any additional services offered through the platform.

Payments may be made using approved payment channels supported by Elimusphere.

Failure to complete payment may result in suspension or restriction of services until payment obligations are met.

6. REFUNDS

Refund requests shall be handled in accordance with the Elimusphere Refund Policy.

Students acknowledge that:

  • Certain digital products may be non-refundable once accessed or downloaded.
  • Refund eligibility may depend on the circumstances of the request.

The complete Refund Policy forms part of this Agreement.

7. STUDENT CONDUCT

Students must use the platform responsibly and respectfully.

Students shall not:

  • Harass, threaten, bully, or intimidate others.
  • Use offensive, abusive, discriminatory, or inappropriate language.
  • Share false information.
  • Disrupt classes or educational activities.
  • Upload harmful software, malware, or viruses.
  • Attempt unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or data.
  • Record, distribute, or publish classes without permission.

Elimusphere reserves the right to remove content, suspend accounts, or terminate access for misconduct.

8. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Students are expected to maintain honesty in all learning activities.

Students shall not:

  • Cheat during assessments.
  • Plagiarize assignments or projects.
  • Submit work completed by another person as their own.
  • Use unauthorized assistance during examinations or evaluations.

Violations may result in disciplinary action, including suspension or termination.
